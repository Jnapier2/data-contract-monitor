# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respectful disagreement, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing another person's private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that do not align with these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant version 2.1.
